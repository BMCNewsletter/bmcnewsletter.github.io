## Introduction

On BendersMC recently, there has been many additions. These additions in may have been major. The new TP protection system is amazing for people looking to not have to file a ticket because they were tp killed. The duel system is amazing in that area too for duels on a new level. Lastly, the new lotus shop is amazing for players who want old seasonals, plushies, and more furniture! These additions just add more to BendersMC as they increase the quality of life on the server.

### TP Protection

On BMC, tp killing has been one of the most notorious and hated acts on the server. Existing on almost every single smp server, it's plagued the enjoyability of Minecraft by making unfair and sudden deaths for countless players.

Luckily, the devs have bmc have added a new feature to significantly reduce this issue. In an announcement on Saturday from the main bmc discord, they have officially added an update that makes players who tp have 1 minute of pvp invincibility, protecting them from any direct attacks from the person they tped to. But, for avid pvpers on the server, this doesn't mean that they will have to wait 1 minute every time they want to start a pvp match. Players can type /tpapvptoggle to permanently turn on and off the invincibility, as well as /pvp to turn off the protection only temporarily to allow pvp matches. This can allow for full flexibility for players interacting with the new feature.

Although this won't prevent all tp kill cases due to indirect killing and group killing still being applicable, this change can help make bmc a safer place, as well as more enjoyable. With this new update, players can finally tp around and have fun in peace, without the luring threat of losing all of their items to a stranger.

In addition to these new updates, the lotus shop has added a new update that can completely revamp the seasonal economy. Players can now buy past seasonals sets for a limited time using lotus coins. Each seasonal set stays in the shop for a few days at a time, before being switched to the next one. This update can provide an advantage to seasonal scalpers, as well as players just wanting to try a taste from the past. Currently, we don't know when each past seasonal set reappearance start and will end, so buy all the seasonals you can before the offer goes away!
To top things off, a new seasonal set has been added to BMC yet again: the anubis set. Playing into Egyptian mythology, the set offers an appealing black and yellow design, as well as a face mask you can wear resembling the god themselves. This set is one of the more fancier ones out of all so far, so seasonal collectors should aim to get it!

### Duel System

The new duel command is a quality-of-life change. This command allows you to duel someone by typing/duel (person), which will initiate a countdown. In this state, no one can attack you or your opponent, so there are no third parties. Now two more modes are added to this duel command. The two modes will be called normal and competitive but let’s dive deeper into this topic.

##### Normal

Do you ever play a game like Marvel Rivals or Fortnite where you have a normal game and a competitive one? Normal mode is just like it but it’s not its own game. Normal mode is a learning curve for new players and people who are trying new elements. Normal doesn’t have a leaderboard so you won’t get embarrassed when you have a low score. But that also means that you won’t be able to flex your score on normal mode either.
##### Competitive

Just like normal mode competitive is a part of the duel command. But unlike normal you gain points every time you beat someone. Every point you get will be shown on the leaderboard so if you aim to be first you have to be experienced in combat in order to get some points. But if you’re trying an element I advise you to stay away from this mode because experienced players might humiliate you.

### Lotus Shop

On BendersMC, the Lotus shop has been a very interesting addition. It has added things such as furniture, player plushies, and now, seasonals.

The new Lotus shop is an actual separated GUI this time, unlike the previous Lotus shop being connected to the /shop GUI. This shop now actually does a lot more. It has daily seasonal rotations, plushies, more furniture, and much more. This new one is a very nice addition to BendersMC. For some, it felt "Like the addition of seasonals" as it has a bunch of new ways to get seasonals. This new way of getting seasonals replaces the vault key in some sense with a cheaper way to get the old seasonals. It has a daily rotation of old seasonals that many wish they had gotten when they were around.
