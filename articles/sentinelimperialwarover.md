# Sentinel Declares Imperial War Over
---

WRITTEN BY ZAK

EDITED BY REIN

---
A conflict that has spanned much of the past year may have finally reached its conclusion at least according to Sentinel leadership.

In an announcement, one of the Sentinel leaders  Reflex stated that the Imperials had effectively retreated from the war, citing over 200 days of inactivity.
The Sentinels no longer see a reason to continue this war as for them it's clear who has won it. 

The announcement was highly critical of the Imperials, claiming that the imperial military dissolved three times. Despite declaring victory, Sentinels stated that it would be open to restarting the conflict in the future if the Imperials wished to organize a battle.

Later on July 3, Imperial leader Emir responded to Sentinel's announcement, making it clear that the Imperials do not consider the war to be over. In his statement, Emir claimed that Elemental Empire was attempting to leave the conflict because they knew the Imperial military had "drastically improved". He also called the  Sentinel both inactive and weak.

Emir further accused EE of originally starting the conflict by betraying the Imperials.
 According to him, the war will continue until EE surrenders.
