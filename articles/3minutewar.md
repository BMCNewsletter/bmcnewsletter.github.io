# The Three Minute War

On June 1, 2026, at approximately 7:34 PM IST, Invictus member Yanyanyve reported in the clan's Discord server that Fiulsi, a Viceroy (high leadership rank) of Elemental Clan (EC), had raided Invictus and attacked several of its members.

The news quickly spread throughout Invictus, prompting clan leader Blood to formally declare war on EC. Funnily enough, this conflict ended just as quickly as it began.

Shortly after the declaration, EC Viceroy Zak opened negotiations with Invictus leadership Blood and Ryu. Representing EC alongside Zak were Plaz, Tijn and Ben. The talks moved swiftly, and within roughly three minutes of the original declaration, both sides reached an agreement.

These were the terms of said pact:
1. Neither EC nor Invictus shall engage in combat against one another, except within public free-for-all (FFA) arenas such as Gerbert.
2. Invictus agrees not to retaliate for the recent attack carried out by Fiulsi.
3. The pact does not constitute an alliance. Neither clan is obligated to provide any form of support to the other.
4. Should either clan initiate an attack against the other outside of an approved FFA arena, the aggrieved clan reserves the right to consider the pact violated and may declare war.

According to those involved in the negotiations, the conflict was called off after it was determined that Fiulsi’s actions had not been authorized by EC leadership. However, as of now, no public action such as demotion or removal from EC has been announced regarding Fiulsi’s conduct, which some may consider suspicious.

With a declaration of war and a peace agreement occurring within only three minutes, this incident has been nicknamed “The Three Minute War” — by far the shortest recent war on BendersMC, significantly more so than the TCE–TDL war that was considered so brief at the time.
