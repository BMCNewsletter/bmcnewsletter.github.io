# A New Era Awaits for Invictus

On June 2, 2026, at 7:46 PM IST, Invictus leader - Blood announced his departure from clan leadership. 

In his depressing announcement, Blood stated personal reasons for stepping down as leader of the clan. While those reasons were shared with the clan, they will not be discussed here out of respect for his privacy. 

The announcement was met with sadness from many members of the clan as Blood had become one of the most recognizable figures within Invictus. 
However, the clan quickly found its new leader. With the support of both the members and Blood himself, Ryu was chosen to take over leadership of the clan.

Fortunately for Invictus, this is not a complete farewell. Blood confirmed that he will continue to play BendersMC when he can, though his presence will likely be less frequent than before.

To honor his contributions Invictus awarded Blood a retired rank, ensuring that his legacy will remain until the clan's end.


 While Blood's departure initially appeared to mark the end of his involvement in Invictus leadership, later developments told a different story. After the personal circumstances that led to his resignation were resolved, Blood returned to the clan.

His return was not without controversy. Shortly afterward, he publicly reported allegations that high-ranking member L Lawliet had been using Freecam ( addition to Minecraft allowing you to go into spectator mode ) and removed him from the clan. The decision sparked debate among members, with some viewing it as justified rule enforcement and others criticizing it as another example of Blood acting on emotion, similar to previous incidents such as the brief EC–Invictus war declaration.

Regardless of opinion, Blood's return ensured that his influence over Invictus did not end with his resignation, adding an unexpected final chapter to what many had believed was the conclusion of an era. Shot
