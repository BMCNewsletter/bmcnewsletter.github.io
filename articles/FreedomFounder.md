# BendersMC Releases Freedom Founder Edition

In celebration of July 4th and the 250th anniversary of the United States, BendersMC has released a new limited-time Founder: Freedom Edition.

The special edition founder includes two exclusive USA-themed tags created by event winners. One tag features the colors of the American flag, while the bald eagle, a national symbol of the United States, inspires the other.
As with all permanent founder ranks, Freedom Founder owners will also receive free seasonal keys each month, giving them access to additional rewards throughout the year
This will be the last special edition founder released for the foreseeable future. 
Once the Freedom Founder leaves the store at the end of July, players will only be able to obtain Founder through the subscription option until another special Founder edition is created.
The rank will remain available until July 31, after which it will be removed from the store.
