# Massive Summer Event

BMC has officially released "L’Été des Éléments", a multi-week summer event packed with daily rewards, competitions, treasure hunts, custom crafting, mini-games, and limited-edition rewards.

The event begins on June 10 running throughout the summer, with different activities unlocking on various dates. Throughout this timeline, Players will be experiencing everything from leaderboard competitions and hidden vaults to gambling mini-games and custom-forged equipment.

One of the event's significant commands is the Summer Calendar, allowing players to claim daily rewards simply by logging in and using 
/summercalendar. Missing a day could mean missing out on exclusive event items and progression rewards.

Competitive pvpers will also be able to participate in the Bending Points Leaderboard, earning points through PvP kills in a race for Silver Ores and a permanent Champion Tag awarded to the first-place finisher.

Explorers have their own challenge through the Vault Hunt, where hidden NPCs will provide riddles leading to four secret vaults scattered across the world. The first player to discover each vault will receive special recognition as its official first finder.

Starting on June 13, players will gain access to Custom Forge Drops, allowing them to collect materials from mobs and craft exclusive event items such as custom armor, wings, and gauntlets through /summonforge.

 On the same day, the BreweryX Event will challenge players to solve riddles and uncover a secret brewing recipe for a one-time reward.
