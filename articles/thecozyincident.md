# The Cozy Incident: A Hoax That Crossed Every Line
---

WRITTEN BY ZAK

EDITED BY REIN

---

On May 28, 2026, at approximately 1:00 AM IST, a disturbing situation unfolded in The Celestial Empire's general chat involving Cozy (IGN: onix_sigma), a well-known builder within the community.

According to the messages sent at the time, Cozy appeared to be experiencing a severe emotional breakdown. He explained that his uncle, someone he described as the kindest person in his life would be leaving for a year or longer. 

What initially seemed like a heartfelt expression of grief quickly escalated into something far more horrifying.

Just five minutes later, Cozy began making statements about ending his life.

The three members who were actively present during the conversation — Amy, Nxyiaa, and Rin immediately attempted to comfort him and convince him not to act on those thoughts. However, the situation took a terrifying turn when Cozy mentioned grabbing a rope.

As Amy, Rin, and Nxyiaa desperately pleaded with him to stop, all communication between him and the three stopped, he wasn't responding at all. 

At 1:22 AM IST, a new message appeared in general chat. Someone claiming to be Cozy's mother began speaking in Filipino. Rin translated the messages for the server, explaining that Cozy's mother had reportedly found his phone on the ground, could not locate him, and had discovered a goodbye note left on his laptop.

The panic intensified when another individual claiming to be Cozy's sister joined the conversation and began discussing the situation as well.

As concern spread throughout the community, BK, the leader of The Celestial Empire, even offered to provide the chat logs to local authorities so that they could assist Cozy's family in locating him.

For many members, the situation appeared tragically real.

It wasn't.

The entire incident had been orchestrated by Cozy and Amy.

BK tried to speak to the alleged "mother" in a voice call in order to help try to locate Cozy, but the request was ignored. He started questioning them in chat. 
The answers to basic questions felt suspicious, and several details failed to add up. As suspicions increased and the story began to unravel, Cozy and Amy eventually confessed.

The alleged suicide attempt, the family members speaking in chat, the goodbye note, and the frantic search were all fabricated by the two. 

According to their confession, the event had been staged to be banned from TCE without anyone else leaving with them (Specifically Gubby and Mex) 

What had been presented as a life-or-death crisis turned out to be an elaborate deception that emotionally manipulated dozens of people who genuinely cared for their well-being.

Several members spent hours awake fearing that a friend had died. Others experienced intense emotional distress while trying to intervene and provide support.

Additional details further undermined the credibility of the story. Based on available information, the drama reportedly began around 3:30 AM Philippine time, raising questions about why both Cozy's mother and sister would have been actively involved in the situation during those early morning hours.

In the aftermath, both Cozy and Amy were permanently banned from The Celestial Empire due to their involvement in the incident.

The event remains one of the most controversial scandals in the community's history not because of what happened, but because of what members were led to believe had happened.

I would like to end this article with a quote Nxyiaa later stated:

"Cozy was sick for starting and going so far with the 'prank' as he so called it. He was way over the line."
