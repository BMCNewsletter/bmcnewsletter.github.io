# The End of D3adforge
---

WRITTEN BY ZAK

EDITED BY REIN

---

Few clans in BendersMC were as hated as D3adforge.

Founded on January 4, D3adforge grew from a small group into a clan with more than 150 members over the course of a year. Despite its growth, the clan was tagged as the most hated clan in all of BMC. 

Much of the criticism stemmed from D3ad's behavior and the controversies surrounding him. One of the most infamous incidents involved D3ad publicly mocking Mystigga for his cancer, while it was later revealed that Mystigga had faked the illness, that information was not known at the time, leading many players to view D3ad's words as deeply insensitive. 
Others criticized his often childish behavior, and with some even joking that a 12-year-old was behind the screen. Over time, the constant criticism appeared to take its toll.

On February 13, 2026, D3ad announced that he would be taking a break from BendersMC.
A few days later, Moonlander — leader of DeadLotus was announced as the clan's new leader. leader but without its founder, D3adforge slowly faded away. Months later, D3ad never returned. Recently, when asked for an invite to the D3adforge Discord server, D3ad gave a simple response:

"d3 is gone"

Instead of providing the server link, he directed attention toward another SMP that he appears to have moved on to. Although the Discord server still exists, the clan itself does not. No announcements and no dramatic ending - just a gradual disappearance.

For a clan that once attracted so much attention, both positive and negative, D3adforge's end was surprisingly quiet. In the end, one of BMC's most hated clans simply faded into history.
