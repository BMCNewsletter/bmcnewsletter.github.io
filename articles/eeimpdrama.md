# Classic Imperial x Elemental Empire drama
---

WRITTEN BY ZAK

EDITED BY REIN

---

On May 30, 2026, at 3:48 AM IST, an Elemental Empire (EE) Emperor Polar publicly addressed the ongoing peace talks between EE and the Imperials. Despite the name, these peace talks weren't so peaceful. 

EE's initial statement accused the Imperials of warmongering, with screenshots allegedly provided as evidence. EE leader Polar also claimed that the Imperials had been avoiding confrontations while simultaneously attempting to keep the war alive and pressure EE into participating in the conflict.

The Imperials soon responded. Imperial Emperor almost released a counter-statement accusing EE of betraying the former Global Defence Coalition (GDC), an alliance consisting of the Imperials, IOM, and TNC. According to Almost, EE abandoned the coalition in favor of UCSR, a faction that GDC had originally been created to oppose. He further claimed that EE ignored multiple opportunities to end the conflict and later violated a peace agreement between GDC and EE.

Polar later issued another statement rejecting the accusations. He argued that the Imperials use the idea of "betrayal" to justify their hostility toward rival clans and claimed EE left the coalition after the Imperials had changed their ideology by allying with racist clans. 
Polar pointed to an alleged incident in which TNC threatened to remove EE from GDC unless it supported a war that TNC had first instigated. Polar also provided some screenshots that served as evidence. 

Polar also highlighted how EE has "ascended" from its relations with the Imperials to flourishing alliances with Sentinels, Grummsh's Kingdom, UCSR, and other FNS nations.

Following Polar's statement,  Almost released a counter-response accusing EE leadership of misleading its members regarding the origins of the conflict.

Almost argued that EE was attempting to simplify two separate wars. He claimed that screenshots shared by EE referenced a conflict involving TNC leader Nova that occurred two years ago and had already ended in a white peace agreement. According to Almost, the current UCSR conflict began later and was initiated by UCSR, which was acknowledged by several EE members themselves. TNC leader Nova doubled down on this claim, stating that the war shown in the screenshots was a separate conflict in which IOM pushed for a white peace despite TNC wanting to continue fighting and force UCSR into admitting defeat.

Almost pointed out  EE's departure from the GDC. He claimed that after leaving the coalition, EE leader Steve dismissed concerns from former allies, as shown through screenshots provided by almost. 

The Imperial leader further accused EE of building its current position through betrayal of former allies rather than genuine growth. He argued that EE's present strength depends heavily on external support from allied factions such as Sentinels and claimed that EE itself is weaker than it was during its time within GDC. He also claimed that the Sentinels do not care about EE and in fact, are using them to fight another clan.
