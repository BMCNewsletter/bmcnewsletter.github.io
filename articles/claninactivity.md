---
Both the Elemental Empire and the Imperial Empire have experienced little activity within their respective clans. Both clans are trying to revive their teams. Many other, smaller clans, are experiencing this issue as well, and most servers are resorting to dead chat pings and kicking inactive members. This is a big deal because the Elemental Empire and the Imperial Empire both are the leading two clans in the server in terms of member count. 

---

### The Elemental Empire inactivity

Currently, the Elemental Empire has barely any of its players on the survival servers. Rapid, a high-ranking member of the empire, said that the clan is dying and that they need to be active again. In addition to this, some EE members are also leaving because of reasons most likely relating to inactivity in the clan. This is not something that is to worry about as the EE is making efforts to revive their clan with one being the Tri Skill Athenlon that they are hosting soon.

---

### Imperial Empire inactivity

Right now in the Imperials, things have been slowing down a bit. Some of the specific divisions in the Imperials such as the build team and PvP team have been extremely inactive. The Imperials have always been an active clan since they came onto the server. They have grown exponentially since they first came on and they look like they are dying. The Imperial leaders are reviving it very fast though and that is a good thing so in general, more active players involved with clans are playing the server again.
