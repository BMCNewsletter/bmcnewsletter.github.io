# New Bending
---

WRITTEN BY REDGAMER

EDITED BY REIN

---

Bending in Bendersmc has been plain recently. There have been no new moves since March. But recently there has been a new update that gives every bending type a new move. Also recently a new sub-element for water bending called steam bending. The main Developer was Steve the emperor of EE.

#### Water
Water the second element anng learnt. There are multiple new moves like Vapor Cloak, Water Shot, Blood Pull, and Sprout. The new move water shot is a chargeable move which can range from 5 blocks to 15 great for low health targets. Sprout needs you to stand on any grass and when fired makes a line of tall grass and flowers. Blood Pull makes enemies come towards you. Vapor Cloak blinds the enemy briefly which is useful for escapes.

#### Fire
The element of life which Zuko describes in the episode The fire bending masters. Like in the book you can learn various moves like Dragon Breath for example. It’s locked behind a level and pay wall to gain these new abilities. Dragons Breath shoots out a powerful burst of fire. But the new move electrocute Shoots out a burst of electricity on the target.

#### Earth
The Third element Anng learnt. This element has a bunch of new moves like the move Golemrush. It makes copper golems attack your target after being shot. Copper nuggets can be used as shrapnel. The new move Armoring can be used to reduce damage from attacks. Just like Toph in book 3 Sozins Comet. 

#### Air
The final element we will be covering is Air. Air has a bunch of new moves like Cloudride. Cloudride is a movement ability that, while sprinting can summon a cloud. Air is also one of the only elements that has a combo. Cyclone is a defensive move that cannot go horizontally only vertically, and can blast away enemies. So far this is the entire new bending update made by Steve except for steam bending.

#### Steam
Steam is a new sub for waterbending. It’s basically cloud-bending. The evaporator turns a water source (or bottle) into steam to freely use. Moves like Cloud Control or Cloud Stream can be activated with the evaporated water. Cloud Control is a bunch of clouds that can be shot at opponents huddling towards them. Steam is a great substitute for any water benders who have access to a portable water source.

In conclusion, water, fire, earth,  and air all got an update. All of them got new moves like Water Shot, Dragon Breath, Armoring, and Cloudride. Air got 2 new combos while water got a new sub-element. Steam is basically cloud-bending which needs a water source or a bottle to activate. Steve did a really good job on this update for the elements.
