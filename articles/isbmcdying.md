# Is BMC Dying?
---

WRITTEN BY ZAK

EDITED BY REIN

---

BendersMC's clan scene appears to be at one of its lowest points in recent times, where dozens of clans once competed for influence. Now, activity has noticeably declined across much of the server.

At any given time, only a handful of clans seem to have active members online simultaneously, while many players remain clanless or inactive. Even some of the server's largest clans have reportedly begun expanding to other servers through the creation of branches, a move some players view as a sign of declining confidence in BMC's future. During a recent visit of mine, only members of EC, Invictus, Sentinel, and the Imperials were noticeably active, with much of the server appearing quiet. 

The moderation team has also become a target of criticism from parts of the active player base, with players accusing them of inefficiency, and unfair decision-making. Whether these criticisms are justified remains a topic of debate. For 2 years, major conflicts particularly the war between the Imperials and Elemental Empire have provided much of the drama and engagement that kept players invested. If that conflict ends, so will BMC. 

While BMC is far from completely abandoned, many players are beginning to ask the same question: "Is BMC dying?"
