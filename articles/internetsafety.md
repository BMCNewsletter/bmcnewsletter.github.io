# Major security update in Litematica
A massive security flaw has been uncovered in Litematica, one of Minecraft’s most popular building mods, with over 20+ million downloads. The vulnerability allows "Infected" servers to compromise a player's computer the moment they log in. 
There are no warnings, pop-ups, or download prompts; simply joining a compromised server is enough to trigger the trap, leaving millions of unsuspecting players at risk.

Once active, the hidden virus can allow hackers to steal account login tokens or access private files on your PC. While the original hacker primarily targeted niche communities, the vulnerability has existed in the mod's code for over a year, meaning anyone running an older version is completely exposed.

To ensure your computer and account are safe, you must update Litematica to its absolute newest version on Modrinth or CurseForge immediately. Developers also recommend opening your local Minecraft folder to manually check for and delete any strange or unfamiliar files before launching the game again.

With the Litematica security flaw making headlines, it's safe to say that the 20+ million Minecraft players have a lot to worry about.

# Rank Scams on the Rise in BendersMC

A growing number of players have reported an increase in rank scams across BendersMC, with several incidents being reported in recent months.

The scam usually follows a simple pattern. Two players agree on a rank trade, with Player A paying the full agreed price upfront. Once the payment has been made, Player B  either delays their end of the trade indefinitely or disappears entirely without providing the promised rank. Buyers are often left with nothing and no one to ask for help from.

What makes these scams particularly forbidden in the community is that they are allowed under server rules, provided no real-world money is involved. Since the trades typically use in-game currency or items rather than actual currency. This has led many players to urge caution when participating in rank trades, recommending the use of trusted, reputable sellers whenever possible.
