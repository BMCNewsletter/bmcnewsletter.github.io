# Formation of Peppa Pig Squad
---

WRITTEN BY ZAK

EDITED BY REIN

---

On the 29th of April, 2026, the leader of The Nova Corporation (TNC), Nova, formally declared war on the Imperium of Man (IoM/Imperium), in what many saw as an attempt to revive TNC.

![Image](https://bmcnewsletter.github.io/images/TNCLogo.webp)

This war caused the temporary alliance of TNC and Sentinel (also joined by Elementals Clan / EC), which was surprising at the time, given that both member clans had been enemies in the past. This temporary coalition destroyed the Imperium militarily.

![Image](https://bmcnewsletter.github.io/image/IOMLogo.webp)

On May 10th, Sentinel left the war coalition as they considered the brief war a victory. They then declared war on TNC, as they were apparently lying to Sentinel about their neutrality in the EE–Imperial war.

On the 16th of May, 2026, the plan for a military pact between the Imperials, IoM and TNC was made public. They named it the Peppa Pig Squad.

However, Nova made it very clear that the Imperium would only be included once they prove their usefulness to TNC and the Imperials — they weren't going to support a dying group. Here are the treaty terms between TNC and IoM:

1. IoM will be given 30 days immediately after the end of the TNC vs Sentinel war to prove themselves useful.
 
2. During this period, no form of hostility will be shown between the two groups.

3. If IoM succeeds in proving themselves as a useful clan, Peppa Pig Squad will be formed.

4. The sole purpose of the Peppa Pig squad will be to grow and expand their personal armies.

5. If IoM doesn't succeed in proving itself useful, then the fate of the war will be decided by TNC's supreme council, with no room for negotiations.

A Sentinel leader, Reflex, was quick to mock the Peppa Pig Squad, indirectly calling it a cheap copy of their own military organisation, the Pretty Princess Brigade. He also pointed out their overwhelming victories against the GDC (the Global Defence Coalition, a former military alliance between TNC, Imps, and IoM) and called it a joke.
