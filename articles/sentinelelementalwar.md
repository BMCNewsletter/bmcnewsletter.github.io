# A friendly war between Sentinels and the Elemental clan
---

WRITTEN BY ZAK

EDITED BY REIN

---
On July 1, Elemental Clan leadership announced the beginning of a friendly war against Sentinel. The conflict was organized independently of the ongoing Sentinel–Imperial rivalry and was intended purely for fun.

According to EC leadership, the decision was made after the continuous delays in the organization of a battle by the Imperial emperor - Almost rather than continue waiting, EC chose to separate itself from the conflict and arrange its own war with Sentinel.

The decision came at an immediate cost. Shortly after the announcement, Imperial leader Almost revoked the alliance between the Imperials and Elemental Clan, bringing year-long relations between the two groups.

Recently On July 7, both Sentinel and Elemental Clan leadership announced the results of their first friendly battle between the two clans.

The final score was a whopping 159–25, with Sentinels securing a victory over EC. Despite the exemplary result, both parties treated the battle lightly and refrained from seriously mocking one another, emphasizing that the conflict was organized purely for fun and practice. 

Instead, both clans mocked the Imperials, pointing out that EC managed to achieve 25 kills in a single hour — something they claimed the Imperials had struggled to accomplish in the recent months. While the score heavily favored Sentinel, the battle remained both friendly and mildly controlled.
