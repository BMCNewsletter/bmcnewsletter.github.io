Pwarp IKEA was a shop on the old Survival that was very iconinc. They sold armor trims, enchanted books, potions, and even mob spawners which used to be able for a player to get but are now just a memory in time. 

The pwarp, IKEA, last existed in 2025 when it had a peak in business. It was built by the player “William Afton” but had support from the leader of the Elemental Empire, “Steve”. 

The pwarp, IKEA, competed with many businesses through pricing and marketing including the iconic pwarp, “!!!Library”! The old survival (Old Survival 1), had many businesses that sold books and other Minecraft necessities. Business during these times could be tough for some.

To go on with these business wars, most involved said it was very brutal. Some of those events included spawning withers at businesses that competed with each other. One of those incidents was with !!!Library planting them at the pwarp, IKEA. Those withers were named

> *“Pw IKEA sucks, people !!!Library is better”*

In conclusion, the Pwarp, IKEA, was a very popular place for players to shop for cheap enchanted books, spawners, and armor trims during its time.
