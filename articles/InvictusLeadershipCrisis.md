# Leadership Crisis Hits Invictus as Blood Steps Down

On July 9th, Invictus leader Blood released a public apology and announced his departure from the clan's leadership.

In his statement, Blood apologized to members he felt he had let down, admitting that he had failed to be the leader Invictus needed.

Shortly afterward, Deb followed up with an announcement explaining everything, he revealed that internal issues had been affecting the clan.  
An emergency leadership meeting had been held due to the internal issues revolving around the clan and shortly after the meeting, Blood had stepped down as leader. 

With Blood gone, Ryu and Deb now assume leadership over the clan


Below is the list of accusations  indicating why Blood has stepped down from leadership:

```1) Waging war on EC

2) Attempting at disbanding Invictus

3) Selling out leadership roles

4) Thinking of selling Kaito's role off to Sidix for 300 million

5) Attempted to make Kaito scam Sidix and wage war

6) Neglected Ryu, the co-leader

7) Attempted to manipulate and bribe Kaito into stating

8) ignored the rest of Invictus leadership```

 However, the story did not end there. Following a public vote within the clan, Blood was reinstated as leader of Invictus. His return marked a dramatic reversal of the leadership crisis and signaled the 
 willingness to give him a second chance. With Blood back as leader, Invictus now looks to move forward from one of the most turbulent periods in its history.

The announcements mark the beginning of a new chapter for 
Invictus.
